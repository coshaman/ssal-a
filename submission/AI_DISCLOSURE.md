# AI-assistance disclosure

The research package was developed with AI assistance for mathematical exploration, code implementation, verification, adversarial review, and manuscript drafting. The human authors are solely responsible for the correctness, originality, citations, proofs, software, and final text. No AI system is listed as an author.

For JGAA, the current author guidance says AI use should be acknowledged in the Comments for the Editors and that authors remain fully responsible. Suggested submission comment:

> Generative AI tools assisted with mathematical exploration, software prototyping, verification support, and language editing. The authors independently checked and take full responsibility for all mathematical claims, citations, code, and final text.
