# Venue report

Status: **Completed for a counting-only paper; publication remains subject to peer review.**

The counting theorem and exact CRT corollary are verified. The sampler is intentionally out of scope, the unconditional bit bound is not claimed, and priority remains conservatively worded because author responses and some database/code surfaces are unavailable.

| ACM Transactions on Algorithms | REACH | Strong algorithms venue, but the present single-DP acceleration and unresolved priority evidence make fit less predictable. Current ACM author requirements must be checked before submission. |

Primary target: **JGAA**. Fallback: **Theoretical Computer Science**. JGAA has the closest scope, explicitly accepts exact graph-algorithm and implementation work, and charges no author fees; TCS is the broader fallback if editors prefer a more general theoretical framing. JGAA's official policy also says that novelty and correctness alone are not sufficient, so the manuscript should foreground the open-problem response and reproducible verification.
