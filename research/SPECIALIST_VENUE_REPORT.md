# Specialist-journal venue report

Date: 2026-09-03 (Asia/Seoul). Fresh official-site/search retrieval was used for the current comparison. This is venue-fit research, not an acceptance prediction.

## Primary recommendation: Journal of Graph Algorithms and Applications (JGAA)

JGAA is the closest topical fit: its official aims scope covers the analysis, design, implementation, and applications of graph algorithms, including exact algorithms. It is diamond open access with no author fees, and its official review description evaluates originality/weight, soundness, scope, presentation, references, and potential to stimulate further research. The paper should be presented as a rigorous exact graph-enumeration algorithm with a clearly delimited arithmetic model. Official pages: https://jgaa.info/index.php/jgaa/about and https://jgaa.info/index.php/jgaa/about/submissions.

## Fallback: RAIRO--Theoretical Informatics and Applications

RAIRO--ITA is a credible fallback for the formal-power-series, enumerative-combinatorics, and theoretical-computer-science aspects. Its official archive shows current volumes and a long-running international publication record. Its official “Randomness and Combinatorics” call explicitly solicited enumerative/analytic combinatorics and random or exhaustive generation, which is thematically adjacent even though the present paper omits the sampler. Sources: https://www.rairo-ita.org/component/issues/ and https://www.rairo-ita.org/news/256-special-issue-of-rairo-ita-on-randomness-and-combinatorics-call-for-papers.

## Other candidates

- Discussiones Mathematicae Graph Theory is a plausible graph-theory fallback; its official article pages show a continuing graph-theory journal with recent volumes and citation metadata. Fit is good for exact enumeration, but the algorithmic/FPS emphasis is less direct than JGAA.
- AKCE International Journal of Graphs and Combinatorics is thematically plausible, but the official site was not reliably fetchable in this environment on 2026-09-03, so no stronger claim about current fees, indexing, or review policy is made.
- Journal of Combinatorial Optimization is a broader hybrid Springer venue. Its official page states that it publishes theoretical, computational, and applied combinatorial-optimization papers and lists DBLP, Scopus, SCIE, and Mathematical Reviews among its indexing services. It is a reasonable stretch target, but the graph-enumeration focus is a closer fit for JGAA/RAIRO--ITA. Source: https://link.springer.com/journal/10878.
- Theoretical Computer Science is retained only as an upper comparison, not as the primary recommendation; the narrow counting-only contribution and conditional bit-complexity scope make a specialist venue more realistic.

## Submission positioning

Emphasize the exact HLV counting problem, the newly exposed coefficient-extraction and EGF-to-linear-ODE reduction, the blockwise `O(n^5 M(n))` finite-field operation bound, and exact CRT recovery. State plainly that the dissertation’s `r=x'+ell'` regrouping and six-argument helper are prior art, and that sampling and unconditional RAM/bit complexity are out of scope.
