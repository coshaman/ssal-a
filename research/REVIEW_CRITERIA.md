# Review criteria

Retrieved 2026-09-03 from official venue pages. The unified conservative rubric is correctness, originality, significance, technical depth, clarity, prior-work positioning, reproducibility, and importance in context of prior work.

- Theoretical Computer Science: current Elsevier policies and guide should govern submission; the venue covers broad theoretical computer science and uses online submission.
- JGAA: scope includes analysis, design, implementation, and applications of graph algorithms. Its policy requires LaTeX/PDF with page and line numbers, DOI-complete references where available, and says new and correct results alone are insufficient; papers should have substantial interest.
- SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics: official scope includes combinatorics, graph theory, and theoretical computer science. Authors submit manuscript and cover letter through the SIAM system; abstracts are limited to 250 words.
- SODA, STOC, FOCS, ICALP, and ESA were considered as selective-conference stress tests. The paper's first pages must establish the problem, prior `O(n^7)` bound, exact new theorem, conceptual mechanism, open-problem relevance, prior-art boundary, and limitations. No conference submission is claimed or prepared.

Primary sources: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/theoretical-computer-science/about/policies, https://jgaa.info/index.php/jgaa/about, https://jgaa.info/index.php/jgaa/about/submissions, https://epubs.siam.org/journal/sidma/instructions-for-authors.
